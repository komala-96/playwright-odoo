## Vendor Invoice Report
  filters: Vendors=chips | Bill Status=select[Open/Closed] | Start Date*=date | End Date=date | Report Type=select[Summary/Detail]
  columns: SAP Vendor # | Vendor | Invoice Date | Vendor Invoice # | Status | Invoice Total | Amount Paid | Balance
## Purchase Order Report
  filters: Vendors=chips | Start Date*=date | End Date=date | Status=select[All/Open/Closed] | Type=select[Summary/Detail]
  columns: Vendor | PO No | Order Date | Ordered Qty | Received Qty | Amount | # of SKUs
## Vendor List Report
  filters: City=chips | State=chips | Activity Status=select[Active/Archived] | 1099 Vendor=input
  columns: Vendor # | SAP # | Vendor Name | Address | Contact Name | Contact Phone # | Credit Limit | Terms
## Inventory Receipt Report
  filters: Type=select[Transaction/Vendor/Category] | Vendor=chips | Product Categories=chips | Start Date*=date | End Date=date
  columns: Vendor # | Vendor | Trans # | Date | Category | Product | Quantity | Cost | Total Amount
## Product Order Report
  filters: Category=chips | Product=chips | As of Date*=date
  columns: Product Code | Product Description | Product Category | Last Purchase Date | Average Weekly Moves | Current Stock | Truck Stock
## Product Transaction Report (Inventory)
  filters: Customers=chips | Products=chips | From Date*=date | To Date*=date | Transaction Type=select[All Types/Bill/Credit Memo/Debit Memo/Direct Invoice/Pack Slip/Purchase/Purchase Return/Quantity Adjustment/Sales Return/Material - Issue/Material - Receipt/Purchase Receipt/Value Adjustment/Store Order]
  columns: Name | Product Code | Product Description | Location | Trans.# | Trans.Date | Transfer Type | Qty | Price | Cost | Amount
